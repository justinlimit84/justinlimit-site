About justinlimit

justinlimit helps Class 9 students learn science by doing it: interactive labs that run in your browser, short animations, and step-by-step solutions that follow the NCERT syllabus.

How we check our work

  • Every definition, symbol, SI unit and sign convention follows the NCERT chapter it belongs to. We don't go beyond the syllabus without saying so ("Beyond the core").
  • Every worked answer is solved twice, by two different methods (the "second way" line under each solution).
  • Every number in the solutions is recomputed by automated tests. The tests also check that the labs obey the textbook formulas: W = F × s, K = ½mv2, U = mgh, P = W/t, MA = L/h, and effort × effort arm = load × load arm. They check that energy is conserved in the slide lab, too.
  • Even so, mistakes can slip through. A quick look by a science teacher is a cheap extra safety net, and we welcome it.

Privacy: we collect nothing

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no forms.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind.
  • Everything (pages, labs, animations, fonts) is served from this site. Nothing is loaded from other companies, so no one else learns that you visited.
  • Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for a short time for security. We don't use them to identify or follow anyone.

Many of our readers are children, so we follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in the simplest way: by not collecting personal data at all.
